## Image Guidelines

1. **Resolution:** Minimum 800x800 pixels for product images
2. **Format:** JPG for photos, PNG for logos/QR codes
3. **Aspect Ratio:** Square (1:1) for product images
4. **Background:** Clean, preferably white or neutral
5. **Quality:** High quality, well-lit photos

## How to Add Images

1. Take high-quality photos of products in good lighting
2. Name the files exactly as listed above
3. Place them in the appropriate folder
4. The website will automatically display them
